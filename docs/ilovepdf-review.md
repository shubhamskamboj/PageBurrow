# iLovePDF tools: pros, cons, and how PageBurrow answers them

Reviewed 20 November 2026 from iLovePDF's own published pages: the home page, the
pricing/limits page, the Terms of Service and the pages of the Split, Page numbers,
Watermark, Sign, Redact, Compare and OCR tools.

**Why there was no hands-on test of iLovePDF itself:** its Terms of Service prohibit using
the service through bots or scripts (clause 5.2(1)) and extracting data from it (8.1(1)).
Automated uploads to every tool would break those terms, so this review uses what iLovePDF
publishes. Points it does not document are marked *not documented*, not assumed. PageBurrow's
own versions were tested hands-on with real files.

## Applies to every iLovePDF tool

**Pros**
- Polished interface that works in any browser, including phones, with nothing to install.
- Opens files from the computer, Google Drive or Dropbox.
- Desktop and mobile apps for paying users.

**Cons**
- **Every file is uploaded to iLovePDF's servers** (company based in Spain). Files are kept
  for up to 2 hours (Terms 9.3). That is a problem for contracts, IDs, medical or bank papers.
- **Needs the internet.** Nothing works offline in the browser version.
- **Free limits per tool**: file size 15–200 MB, and as little as 1 file per task for the
  Office, OCR, PDF/A and repair tools (pricing page).
- **Paywalls**: OCR conversions, PDF/A, split by size, smart split and redaction, digital
  signatures and the AI tools need Premium or AI credits.
- **Automation is forbidden** for normal users (Terms 5.2), and workflows are limited on the
  free plan ("1 x free").

**PageBurrow, for all tools**: everything runs on your own device; no upload, no
internet, no account, no limits other than your device's memory, no ads, nothing paid.
Workflows are unlimited.

## Not possible offline (stated plainly)

- **Digital (certificate-based) signatures and signature requests to other people.** These
  need a certificate authority or an online service. PageBurrow adds visual signatures.
- **AI-written summaries and AI translation.** These need large AI models or an online
  service. PageBurrow uses offline methods instead (see rows 31 and 32).

signatures only.
