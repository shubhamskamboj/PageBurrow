PAGEBURROW: EVERY PDF TOOL, ON YOUR OWN COMPUTER
================================================

Edit, merge, split, compress, convert, sign, protect and OCR PDFs in your web browser.
Everything happens on this computer: nothing is uploaded, there is no account, and no
internet is needed. There is nothing to install: Python and the PDF engine are included.


HOW TO START

Windows 10 or 11
  1. Right-click PageBurrow.zip, choose "Extract All", then open the extracted
     PageBurrow folder. (Starting it from inside the zip does not work.)
  2. Double-click  Start-Windows.cmd
     If Windows warns about a file from the internet, choose "More info", then
     "Run anyway".

Mac (macOS 11 or later, Apple silicon or Intel)
  1. Double-click PageBurrow.zip to unpack it, then open the PageBurrow folder.
  2. Double-click  Start-macOS.command
     The first time, macOS may block it:
     - macOS 14 or older: right-click the file, choose Open, then Open.
     - macOS 15 or newer: close the message, open System Settings >
       Privacy & Security, and click "Open Anyway".

Linux (x86-64 or ARM64)
  1. Unpack PageBurrow.zip.
  2. Double-click  start-linux.sh  (choose "Run in Terminal" if asked),
     or run:  sh start-linux.sh


PageBurrow then opens in your web browser at http://127.0.0.1:8765
Keep its window (Command Prompt or Terminal) open while you work, and close it to
stop PageBurrow. Download your PDFs before you stop it.


PHONES AND TABLETS

On the computer, start Start-Windows-Phone-Access.cmd (or the macOS or Linux
"phone access" file) instead. Its window shows an address and a 6-digit PIN to open
on a phone or tablet on the same Wi-Fi. See Start-iPhone-iPad.txt.
